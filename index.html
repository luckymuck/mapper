About Mapper

Mapper is a private, local visualizer for your location history. It turns Google Takeout, photo GPS, CSV/GPX, and typed places into maps of where you've been — visited regions, trails, trips, and a heat grid.

Runs locally. Your files and location data never leave this device. Everything is processed in your browser.

What's shared: to show county boundaries for countries outside the bundled set, Mapper optionally fetches them from geoBoundaries and sends the list of visited country codes to a CORS proxy (allorigins). Map tiles come from Esri / OpenStreetMap, and place lookups use OpenStreetMap Nominatim. Basemap © OpenStreetMap contributors.

Boundary data: Natural Earth / geoBoundaries (CC BY 4.0). Built with Leaflet, JSZip, exifr, and rbush.

Add points

One per line: a place name or coordinates, then an optional date. Examples:
Los Angeles, California, USA 8/15/2026 — also accepts 8/15/26 or 15 Aug 2026
34.0522, -118.2437 2026-08-15
Paris, France 15 Aug 2026
25°04'50.0"N 121°13'37.3"E

For best results, use City, Country (e.g. "Bangkok, Thailand"). If a place isn't found, the search progressively simplifies — you don't need to do anything.

Add trip

Name the trip and set a start/end date, then list the stops (a place or coordinates, one per line). Dates are spread evenly across the stops; a per-line date overrides that stop's date. For best results use City, Country (e.g. "Chiang Mai, Thailand").
